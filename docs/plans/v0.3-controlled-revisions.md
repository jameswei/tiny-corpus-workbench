# v0.3 — Controlled Revisions

## Outcome

v0.3 added controlled refinement for one supported diagnosis finding. A human
decision produces either no prepared document after rejection or one immutable
successor revision after approval.

Fixed deterministic refiners preserve the original source, canonical `orig`
data, provenance, stable document references, lineage, and append-only
transformation history. Approved revisions record forward derivation and exact
reversal evidence. A successor must be diagnosed again before another proposal
is drafted.

## Rationale and boundaries

The milestone made mutation an explicit, inspectable act. A finding does not
authorize a change, and a requested proposal does not prove approval or
application. Rejection and approval both preserve the evidence that led to the
decision.

The supported refiners are narrow and mechanical. v0.3 did not add semantic
rewrites, automatic repair, batch refinement, a browser or service interface,
or downstream RAG work. It never overwrites a source, observation, diagnosis,
or prior revision.

## Validation outcome

The released workflow verified rejection without a prepared document,
approval with one successor, stable lineage, exact forward and reverse
derivation, tamper detection, and immutable publication.

## Later direction

The final v0.5 design simplified persisted authority:
`refinement-manifest.json.decision` is the only persisted structured decision
authority. Proposal state and transformation state remain lifecycle facts.
Current refinement behavior belongs to the
[Controlled Revisions guide](../controlled-revisions.md).
