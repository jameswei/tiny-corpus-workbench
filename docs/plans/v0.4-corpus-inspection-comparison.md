# v0.4 — Corpus Inspection and Comparison

## Outcome

v0.4 added inspection of one explicit, small local corpus. It processes listed
members sequentially through observation and diagnosis. A corpus can also
compare fully verified applied revisions as read-only inputs.

The run publishes deterministic, source-text-free JSON and a static offline
HTML report. It continues through individual member failures, records the
incomplete evidence, and publishes the corpus atomically.

## Rationale and boundaries

The milestone made cross-document patterns visible without hiding member
evidence or creating a batch mutation system. Inputs are explicit: the CLI
does not discover directories, follow URLs, read stdin, or silently select
revisions.

Corpus inspection does not refine or mutate a member. The report does not
embed arbitrary source or prepared-document passages. The golden matrix teaches
format and document-family comparison. The separate quality corpus teaches the
selected diagnosis conditions.

v0.4 did not add automatic repair, concurrent orchestration, a live browser
application, a hosted service, or downstream RAG work.

## Validation outcome

The released workflow verified deterministic aggregation, member-failure
continuation, atomic publication, source-text-free reports, corpus and revision
integrity, the three-family four-format golden matrix, and the focused quality
corpus.
